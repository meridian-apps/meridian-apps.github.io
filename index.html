Meridian Apps

Garmin Connect IQ apps for rucking, weighted-vest walks, and loaded hikes.

RuckTrack

The full rucking app

A dedicated Connect IQ activity app: multiple live screens, on-watch load presets, post-activity summaries, and load-aware calories via the Pandolf equation. For when you want rucking to be its own thing on the watch.

PackCal

Vest & pack calories, one data field

A single data field you drop into a Hike, Walk, or Ruck you already use. It adds the calories your vest or pack actually costs, with the same Pandolf math as RuckTrack and calories as the hero of the cell. The lightweight option.
